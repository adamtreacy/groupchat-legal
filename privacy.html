Groupchat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account info: name, handle, email, avatar photo.
  • Identity verification data: when you verify to become a chat member, our verification provider (Stripe Identity) collects your government-issued ID and a live selfie to confirm you're a real, matching person. Groupchat does not store the raw ID image or biometric data ourselves — Stripe processes and retains it under their own privacy policy; we only store the verification result (verified / not verified) and the verified name for matching against your account.
  • Content: messages, reactions, chat memberships.
  • Usage data: app interactions, device/browser type, crash logs.

2. How we use it

  • To operate the App (show chats, post messages, enforce membership).
  • To verify you're a real person before letting you post (fraud/bot prevention).
  • To moderate content and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To improve the App (aggregated usage analytics).

3. Who we share it with

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) — our infrastructure provider.
  • Stripe Identity — for identity verification only.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the safety of users (e.g. reporting CSAM to NCMEC/eSafety Commissioner).

4. Where data is stored

Data may be processed and stored in the United States, regardless of your location, due to our infrastructure providers.

5. Your rights

United States (e.g. California/CCPA): You may have the right to know what personal data we hold, request deletion, and opt out of sale of data (we don't sell data). Contact us to exercise these rights.

Australia (Privacy Act 1988 / Australian Privacy Principles): You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request correction, and lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you believe we've mishandled your data.

6. Data retention

Account and content data is retained while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we're required to retain it (e.g. records of moderation/legal actions, verification results needed to prevent re-abuse).

7. Children's privacy

Groupchat is not intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls via Supabase Row Level Security) to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll notify users of material changes to this policy by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@groupchat.app.
